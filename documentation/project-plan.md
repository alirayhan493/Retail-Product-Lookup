# Retail Product Lookup Application

## Problem

While working as a retail cashier, I frequently encountered items that were missing price tags, barcodes, or product labels. Without a way to lookup these items at the register, employees, often had to leave their station to search the sales floor to ask another assoicate for help. This process slowed down the checkout, increased customer wait times, and created unneceassary frustration for both employees and customers.

## Proposed Solution

Develop a mobile application that allows retail employees to quickly search and access product information from a centrailized database.

Employees can search for prducts by name and descript and pull up important details such as pricing, prduct images, and item information.

## Project Goal

The goal of this application is to reduce the time employees spend searching for product information and improve efficiency during customer interaction.

## Core Features

-Search products by name or SKU
-Filter products by category
-View product details
-Store Product information in a database
-Upload and display product images
-Manage product records through CRUD operations
